systems designer

I redesign how teams and people work, with creative direction, custom tools and a few good rules.

what I do

✶ the short version

I look at how a business or a person actually works, find where time and attention leak, and build the structure that fixes it — a pipeline, a tool, a rule. I care about changes that pay off over the long run, not quick patches.

who it's for

✶ teams & people
  • for teams — workflows, pipelines, custom tools and working principles, built around how your team really operates.
  • for people — consulting on personal systems: routines and life setups that fit how you actually live.

how I work

✶ the process
  • listen — I pay attention to how work really happens: the meetings, the calls, the handoffs.
  • find the leak — where time, attention or momentum quietly gets lost.
  • design the smallest fix — a rule, a ritual or a tool, as small as it can be while still working.
  • make it stick — build habits around it, like a fifteen-minute look every morning, so it survives a busy week.

what I've built

✶ a few examples
  • a video pipeline — for my former agency, set up on monday.com and frame.io.
  • a custom tracking system — coded for the architecture office I'm currently at, with its own rules built in, like a fifteen-minute look at it every morning when the day opens.
  • working principles — after listening to a company's meetings, I suggest small rules. One phone-heavy company's calls kept running long, so every call now ends with the same closing line: “how can we do it faster?”
  • creative direction & execution — I set the creative direction for NotWork and carry it out myself.
