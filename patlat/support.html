Common questions

I bought Premium but it's not showing as unlocked.

Open the menu (the icon top-right) and tap the Premium pill, then Restore Purchases. This re-checks your Apple/Google account for a purchase that's already been made and re-applies it on this device.

How do I turn off reminder notifications?

Notifications for reminders you've created can be turned off in your device's own system Settings for Patlat, under Notifications.

How do I delete a headspace, or all my data?

Open the menu → Settings → Headspaces to remove a single headspace, or Reset everything at the bottom of Settings to erase all bubbles, headspaces, and reminders on this device.

Does my data sync between devices?

Not yet — everything in Patlat is stored only on the device you're using. See our Privacy Policy for details.

I want a refund.

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google, so refunds have to go through them directly — we can't issue refunds ourselves. On iOS: reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android: Google Play Store → Order History → find Patlat → Request a refund.
