Privacy Policy

Effective September 17, 2026

The short version: Patlat doesn't collect your data. Everything you write — every bubble, headspace, and reminder — stays on your device. We don't have a server that sees it, and we don't run analytics or ad trackers of any kind.

Information we don't collect

Patlat has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no cloud sync. We don't use analytics software, advertising SDKs, or tracking pixels of any kind. We don't know who you are, what you write in your bubbles, or how you use the app — because none of that ever leaves your device.

Information stored on your device

Everything you create in Patlat is stored locally, using your device's own on-device storage:

  • Your bubbles (tasks) and the headspaces you organize them into
  • Calendar reminders you create
  • Photos you attach to a bubble
  • Your app preferences (popping mode, sound/haptics, cosmetics, language)

None of this is transmitted to us or to any third party. If you delete the app, or use Reset everything in Settings, this data is gone for good — we have no copy of it to restore.

Permissions Patlat asks for

iOS and Android will ask your permission before Patlat can use any of the following. Each is used only for the feature described, entirely on your device:

  • Camera — only when you choose to take a photo to attach to a bubble.
  • Photo Library — to let you pick an existing photo for a bubble, or save one you've added back to your library.
  • Motion & Fitness — to detect when you shake your phone, which jiggles the bubbles on screen.
  • Notifications — to send you the reminders you've scheduled in the Calendar tab. These are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device.

In-app purchases

Patlat offers an optional one-time Lifetime purchase that unlocks premium features. This purchase is processed by Apple's App Store (or Google Play on Android) — Patlat never sees your payment details, name, or Apple/Google account information.

To validate and manage your purchase, Patlat uses RevenueCat, a third-party subscription/purchase-management service. RevenueCat may receive an anonymous, app-generated device identifier and the purchase/transaction data needed to confirm your entitlement. It does not receive your name, email address, or payment information. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle this data.

Children's privacy

Patlat is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since Patlat collects no personal information from anyone, this is true for users of every age.

Data deletion

Because Patlat keeps everything on your device and nothing on a server, there is no remote copy of your data for us to delete. You are always in full control:

  • Use Settings → Reset everything to erase all bubbles, headspaces, and reminders on this device.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its local data as well.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above and post the new version at this same address. We won't make changes that reduce your rights without clear notice.

Contact us

Questions about this policy, or about your data? Reach out any time.
